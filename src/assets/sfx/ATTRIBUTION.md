Edits: excerpts selected; mono conversion; high/low-pass filtering; mild FFT
noise reduction; loudness normalization (-23 to -27 LUFS); short fades; MP3 96 kbps.
Fox excerpts: 5.55–6.20 s and 15.45–16.10 s. Purr: 3.50–6.60 s.
Pitches and speeds are unchanged. The greeting and startled-cat interactions use
short recorded mews rather than claiming to be separately recorded trills/yowls.
Reveal cue is synthesized in src/sfx.ts, with no third-party recording.
